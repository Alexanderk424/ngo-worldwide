How the Enquiry Process Works

  1. Contact our support center with your enquiry.
  2. Provide only the shipment information necessary to assess your request.
  3. Ask for the provider's identity, service availability, costs, and terms before agreeing to proceed.
  4. Confirm the arrangement directly with the responsible provider before sending any parcel or payment.
Important service notice: Publishing this information does not mean that shipping, parcel collection, shipment tracking, or delivery services are already active. Confirm provider arrangements, prices, delivery terms, and coverage before advertising or accepting a booking.
